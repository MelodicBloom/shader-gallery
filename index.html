What this is aiming for

Growth here means a larger catalog of materials and rising mechanical accuracy: effects that respond correctly when the observer angle or light-source direction changes, instead of baking a single hero look.

The work targets known phenomenological mechanisms—subsurface scattering, layered angle-dependent spectral diffraction / thin-film interference, Fresnel and thickness-coupled response, roughness and metalness coupling, transmission and dispersion—expressed as optical parameters with explicit relationships and controlled scenarios (neutral view, grazing incidence, light sweep).

Current: interactive artistic optical studies and open validation trials. Not claimed yet: measured or calibrated physical accuracy. A compile or a pretty frame is not a pass.

Material research lineup

Three pilots are contracted in the Material Validation Lab. Validation runs in sequence because each receipt unlocks the next decision; the live demos above can be explored in parallel anytime.

  1. 1 · Active Abalone / nacre baseline (MVT-ABALONE-001) — Khronos glTF iridescence maps under controlled light and view; channel semantics before any procedural nacre invents thickness. Anchors map–lighting relationships (intensity vs thickness channels, observer polar angle, key-light azimuth).
  2. 2 · Next Soap-bubble spectral thin film (MVT-BUBBLE-001) — Two-beam spectral / Fresnel treatment vs an RGB-proxy control; phase-consistent hue/intensity with thickness and incident/view angle. Decides whether spectral integration earns its cost.
  3. 3 · Queued Gold-foil field study (MVT-GOLD-001) — Original deterministic fold-height fields coupled to normal and roughness; directional highlight breakup that must survive three light directions, not a single hero pose.
Parallel vs sequence. Demos and hypothesis shaders can run side by side. Formal trials stay ordered: Abalone map semantics → Bubble spectral vs proxy → Gold foil lighting response → only then a broader Three.js / R3F adapter decision. Parallel trial execution would only make sense after Abalone’s channel/lighting receipt is no longer the blocker for the others.
