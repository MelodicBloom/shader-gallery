HELIOS RELAY

Kinetic light vault

Starting

Pointer on the vault sets aperture and energy.

Aperture hinges all eight ribs from 0° to 65°. Energy sets signal gain from 0.15 to 1.10 and does not move the structure. Yaw turns the whole vault. Phase scrubs the 12 second light cycle. Drag horizontally for aperture and vertically for energy. Two touches: separation sets aperture, angle sets yaw. Keys: arrows, brackets, comma, period, space, R.
